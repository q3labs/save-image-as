# Privacy policy: Q3 Save Image As – JPG, PNG, WebP

*Last updated: 22 October 2026*

**We collect no data. Your images never leave your browser.**

## What the extension does

When you right-click an image and choose a format, the extension downloads that one image from the website it came from, converts it on your device, and hands the converted file to your browser's download manager.

## What we collect

Nothing. The extension has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no advertising and no remote code. We never receive your images, the addresses of the pages you visit, or anything else about you.

## Network requests

The only network request the extension makes is the one that fetches the image you right-clicked, from the same website that shows it. Nothing is sent to Q3 Labs or to any third party. The "More free tools" button opens q3labs.co in a normal browser tab, and only when you click it.

## What is stored

Three settings are stored with your browser's sync storage: your default format, the JPG/WebP quality, and whether to show the save dialog. They contain no personal data. If you are signed in to a browser that syncs, the browser may sync them between your devices; that is done by the browser, not by us.

## Permissions

Each permission exists only to do the job described above. The extension reads images only after you choose "Save image as…" from the right-click menu on that image. See `STORE_LISTING.md` for the reason behind each permission.
